# SpecFlowLab 1.0.7

SpecFlowLab 1.0.7 advances the AI Investigation and global-analysis workflow
with evidence-first organization, focused fsTA signature review, and more
predictable reset behavior.

## Highlights

- Edit automatic and user-defined EAS signatures directly from plot labels or
  the Global Fit Summary table. Automatic suggestions are limited to the two
  strongest noise-aware candidates per EAS component.
- Keep external datasets, spectroscopy and characterization files, figures,
  manuscripts, and literature in a file-first Evidence Workspace with explicit
  dataset connections and a relation-map view.
- Reset an active dataset or a full folder without leaving baseline or chirp
  state behind; reset fit inputs independently while retaining the last result.
- Navigate spectrum and kinetics slices more smoothly through animation-frame
  coalescing and targeted chart repainting.
- Review larger EAS and DAS previews while keeping IRF-limited components out of
  interpreted plots, feature labels, exports, and evidence summaries.
- Export Origin heatmaps with wavelength across columns, log-time from 0.1 ps,
  eight major and five minor color levels, and no residual graph.

## Scientific boundary

Detected bands and GSB/SE/ESA assignments remain `suggested-not-confirmed`
candidate evidence. EAS is a sequential-model preview, not independent proof of
a species spectrum. Raw matrices, fit diagnostics, provenance, and residual
data remain available for audit even where residual graphs are intentionally
omitted from presentation output.
