# Code of Conduct

SpecFlowLab is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.
Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of
different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private information.
Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report a concern privately to specflowlab@icluod.com.
